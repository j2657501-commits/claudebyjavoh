Темы актуального сезона: сентябрь–декабрь 2026. Списки составлены по сообщениям кандидатов (официально IELTS вопросы не публикует), поэтому на экзамене формулировка может немного отличаться. Карточки с пометкой NEW появились в этом сезоне.

ЧастьВремяЧто происходитКак отвечать
Part 14–5 минВопросы о тебе: работа/учёба, дом + 2 бытовые темы2–3 предложения: ответ + причина + пример
Part 23–4 минКарточка: 1 мин на подготовку, 1–2 мин монологГовори до остановки экзаменатора; иди по пунктам карточки
Part 34–5 минАбстрактные вопросы по теме карточки4–6 предложений: мнение → причина → пример → оговорка

Критерии (по 25%): Fluency & Coherence · Lexical Resource · Grammatical Range & Accuracy · Pronunciation.

Полезные фразы

  • Выиграть время: That's an interesting question. / I've never really thought about it, but… / Let me think…
  • Мнение: As far as I'm concerned… / I'd say that… / From my point of view… / I tend to think…
  • Пример: For instance… / Take my brother, for example… / A good example of this is…
  • Сравнение прошлого и настоящего: Compared with the past… / People used to…, whereas nowadays… / It's much more common now to…
  • Оговорка: Having said that… / It depends on… / On the other hand… / That's not always the case, though.
  • Part 2, начало и конец: I'd like to talk about… / The reason I chose this is… / All in all, it was… / Looking back, I think…
  • Если не понял: Sorry, could you repeat that, please? / Do you mean…?

Схема ответа Part 2 на 2 минуты

  1. 10 сек — что/кто это (I'd like to tell you about…)
  2. 30 сек — фон: когда, где, как ты об этом узнал
  3. 50 сек — детали: что происходило (Past Simple / Past Continuous / Past Perfect)
  4. 30 сек — чувства и почему это важно; что изменилось (If I hadn't…, I would never have…)

Образец ответа Part 1 (Band 8)

Do you often feel bored? — Not very often, to be honest. I've got quite a full timetable at university, so there isn't much room for boredom. If it does happen, it's usually when I'm stuck somewhere with nothing to do, like waiting in a long queue at the bank.

Do you wear a watch? — I used to, but I stopped a couple of years ago because I check the time on my phone anyway. Having said that, I still keep the watch my grandfather gave me, and I put it on for special occasions.

Образец ответа Part 2 (Band 8)

Describe a time when you saved some money to buy something special.

I'd like to talk about the time I saved up for my first proper laptop, which was about three years ago, when I was in my final year of school.

At that time I was using an old family computer that froze every ten minutes, and I'd just become interested in video editing, so I desperately needed something faster. The model I had my eye on cost roughly six hundred dollars, which was a fortune for a teenager, and my parents made it clear that if I wanted it, I'd have to earn it myself.

So I came up with a plan. I started tutoring two younger kids from my neighbourhood in maths, twice a week, and I also cut back on little things like snacks and taxi rides. I kept the cash in an envelope at the back of my drawer and wrote the running total on it, which was surprisingly motivating. It took me almost eight months, and there were moments when I was tempted to spend the money on a new phone instead, but I managed to resist.

When I finally walked into the shop and paid in cash, I felt incredibly proud. Honestly, the laptop itself wasn't the best part. What mattered was knowing that I'd bought it with money I had earned. Looking back, I think that experience taught me more about patience and budgeting than any lesson at school, and I still take better care of that laptop than of anything my parents have ever bought me.
